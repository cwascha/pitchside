Everything in one place

Built for how youth clubs actually run.

Not a general-purpose HR tool adapted for sports. Purpose-built for clubs that run weekend training programs with 10–80 trainers.

Trainer scheduling
Assign trainers to sessions from your available pool. The system tracks who worked which weekends and surfaces the right people first.
SMS confirmations
Trainers get a text with their session details and training plan link. They reply YES or NO. That's the whole flow — no app required.
Automatic payroll
Hours tracked from session times. Sparks program premium applied automatically. Export a CSV pay report for any date range in one click.
Mobile attendance
After each session, open a signed link on your phone. Tap any trainer who didn't show. No-shows drop out of payroll instantly — no login needed.
Training plan delivery
Upload your weekly plans for each program. Links go out automatically with every assignment SMS — the right plan to the right trainer.
W9 & compliance
See at a glance which trainers are missing W9s, have unpaid balances, or need follow-up. No more hunting through folders before tax season.
